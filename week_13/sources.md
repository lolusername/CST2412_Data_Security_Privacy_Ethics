# Week 13 Sources and Research Anchors

Research checked on May 3, 2026.

## Course anchors
- `CST_2412.pdf`
  The syllabus lists Week 13 as **Data Poisoning and Model Evasion**.

- Pfleeger, C. P., Pfleeger, S. L., & Margulies, J. (2015). *Security in Computing* (5th ed.). Pearson.
  Useful connections include security controls, intrusion detection limits, data integrity, privacy, and risk management.

## External anchors
- NIST, **Adversarial Machine Learning: A Taxonomy and Terminology of Attacks and Mitigations**  
  https://www.nist.gov/publications/adversarial-machine-learning-taxonomy-and-terminology-attacks-and-mitigations  
  Used for the week vocabulary around adversarial machine learning, poisoning, evasion, privacy breach, and mitigation.

- NIST, **AI Risk Management Framework**  
  https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework  
  Used to connect AI/security risks to broader risk management practices.

- OWASP, **Machine Learning Security Top Ten**  
  https://owasp.org/www-project-machine-learning-security-top-10/  
  Used as a beginner-readable security framing for machine learning risks.

- OWASP, **ML02:2023 Data Poisoning Attack**  
  https://owasp.org/www-project-machine-learning-security-top-10/docs/ML02_2023-Data_Poisoning_Attack  
  Used to explain how manipulated training data can cause model behavior to become unreliable or harmful.
