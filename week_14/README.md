# Week 14: Final Project Studio and Privacy Incident Detective

## Week focus
Week 14 combines final-project support with one new practical wrap-up topic.

Day 1 helps students turn evidence into findings. Day 2 shifts into a fun, fictional privacy incident investigation that connects the syllabus themes of data security, cloud sharing, access control, privacy, ethics, and governance.

## Final project connection
The final project is due **May 26, 2026**.

Students are **not giving a presentation**. Week 14 replaces presentation prep with individual project development and an individual incident-response lab.

## Work mode
All Week 14 activities are **individual**.

## Recommended class flow

### Day 1: Turning Evidence Into Findings
1. Mini lesson: how to turn data, sources, and notebook outputs into findings
2. Sample project walkthrough: security metrics daily summary
3. Individual lab: students draft their project question, evidence list, findings, recommendation, and privacy/ethics paragraph
4. Checkpoint submission

### Day 2: Privacy Incident Detective
1. Mini lesson: how to investigate a fictional privacy incident without overclaiming
2. Individual case lab: students classify exposed data, build a timeline, score severity, choose first-hour response actions, and write a plain-language incident update
3. Short final-project bridge: students connect the incident method to their own evidence, recommendation, or limitation if useful
4. Exit ticket: students name the key fact, risky assumption, response action, prevention control, and final-project connection
