# Week 11 Day 2 Career-Connected Writing Response

## Role
You are a **Tier 1 security analyst** writing an escalation memo to the incident lead.

## Time
10-15 minutes

## Length
220-300 words

## Prompt
Using the alert-triage lab, write a short escalation memo that answers this question:

**Which alert should be escalated first, and why does it deserve attention before the others?**

## Your memo must include
1. the `alert_id`
2. one sentence on severity and confidence
3. one sentence on asset context
4. one recommended next step
5. one sentence explaining why analyst judgment still matters beyond any simple score
6. one sentence acknowledging a privacy or monitoring boundary

## Suggested structure
- `Escalated alert:`
- `Why this alert stands out:`
- `Recommended next step:`
- `Why judgment matters:`
- `Privacy note:`

## Career connection
This kind of writing is close to what an entry-level:
- SOC analyst
- security analyst intern
- incident triage analyst
- junior threat analyst
might produce in a real environment.

## Grading criteria
- clear prioritization logic
- correct use of severity, confidence, and asset context
- practical next step
- professional tone
- explicit recognition that analytics must be used responsibly
