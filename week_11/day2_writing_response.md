# Week 11 Day 2 Short Exit Memo

## Role
You are a **Tier 1 security analyst** writing a quick escalation note.

## Time
5-8 minutes

## Length
120-170 words

## Prompt
Using your Day 2 lab work, answer this question:

**Which alert should be escalated first, and why?**

## Your note must include
1. the `alert_id`
2. one sentence about why the alert is urgent
3. one sentence about why the asset context matters
4. one recommended next step
5. one short privacy or monitoring-boundary sentence

## Suggested structure
- `Escalated alert:`
- `Why it matters:`
- `Next step:`
- `Privacy note:`

## Career connection
This is similar to the short notes written by:
- SOC analysts
- security interns
- incident triage analysts

## Grading criteria
- clear alert choice
- clear reason for urgency
- correct use of asset context
- practical next step
- one explicit monitoring-boundary sentence
