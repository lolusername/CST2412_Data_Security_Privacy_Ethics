# Week 11 Day 1 Career-Connected Writing Response

## Role
You are a **junior SOC analyst** writing a short shift note to your team lead.

## Time
10-15 minutes

## Length
200-280 words

## Prompt
Using the evidence from today's CSV lab, write a short professional shift note that answers this question:

**Which authentication pattern should your team investigate first, and why?**

## Your note must include
1. one suspicious pattern from the data
2. one exact piece of evidence from the CSVs
3. one reason account context matters
4. one recommended first action
5. one sentence showing awareness of privacy or data-minimization concerns

## Suggested structure
- `Summary:` what pattern you found
- `Evidence:` what rows or fields support your concern
- `Action:` what you recommend first
- `Privacy note:` what should still be handled carefully

## Career connection
This is the kind of short writing an entry-level:
- SOC analyst
- security operations intern
- incident response analyst
- detection analyst
might need to do during a real shift.

## Grading criteria
- clear professional tone
- evidence-based reasoning
- defensible first action
- correct use of account context
- explicit privacy awareness
