# Week 12 Sources and Research Anchors

Research checked on April 30, 2026.

## Course anchors
- `CST_2412.pdf`
  The syllabus lists Week 11-12 as **Data Analytics for Security**.

- Pfleeger, C. P., Pfleeger, S. L., & Margulies, J. (2015). *Security in Computing* (5th ed.). Pearson.
  Useful textbook connections include audit logs, intrusion detection, database security, data mining, privacy risk, and privacy-preserving data mining.

## External research anchors
- NIST SP 800-92 Rev. 1 Initial Public Draft, **Cybersecurity Log Management Planning Guide**  
  https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/92/r1/ipd  
  Used to support the idea that logs are generated, stored, analyzed, and disposed of for security operations, incident investigation, operational troubleshooting, and retention needs.

- NIST SP 800-181, **NICE Cybersecurity Workforce Framework**  
  https://www.nist.gov/document/nistsp800-181pdf  
  Used to connect beginner analytics work to cybersecurity work roles and security/privacy insight.

- NIST Privacy Framework  
  https://www.nist.gov/privacy-framework  
  Used to frame privacy as enterprise risk management and to remind students that security analytics should include privacy thinking.
